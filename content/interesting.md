# Austin Fay

*Austin is a tinkerer and builder interested in the future of American Energy and how humans make decisions.*

## Current Focus

Building an ai-native tool for royalty ownership recovery, Bakken division order analysis, and tinkering with whatever catches curiosity. Linux has been in my workflow for a decade. Currently flying EndeavourOS.

The work happens at the intersection of software, systems, and daily practice — building things that work, fixing things that don't, and learning from both.

I'm also very into the writings and ideas of Jung. I recently completed a course at the [McMillan Institute for Jungian Studies](https://www.junghouston.org/jungian-studies#UpcomingEvents) on the Puer Aeternus.

## Influence me with

- Systems thinking
- Pragmatic design
- Reverse Engineering 
- Concise writing
- Cognitive Science 
- Subversive thinkers
- Linux Distros
