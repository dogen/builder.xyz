That reframes the whole thing. The prior reviews read the container-vs-contents gap as avoidance — a specific resistance to exposure. The garden isn't being avoided so much as it isn't yet load-bearing in the day. That's a legitimate state. The failure mode isn't "you're not writing"; it's paying the overhead of a public, staged, reviewed system for something you're relating to as a private scrapbook.

- **A public garden.** Then publishing is the point, and the ceremony has to actually move — start with Top-3 item #1.

What genuinely has momentum, still: **the professional side and the physical builds** — Btrail and the XR200. Both are real, both have artifacts, and both live entirely outside this vault. That's the tell. The neglect isn't of the interests; it's of the *recording of them here specifically*. The vault is downstream of a life that's happening elsewhere and not flowing in.

## Workflows to build

- **Right-size this review.** **Trigger:** now. **Action:** if the vault's honest change-rate is monthly, move this job to **monthly** (or event-triggered: "review when ≥N new entries land since the last one"). **Payoff:** stops a weekly cron from narrating stasis, and makes each review land against actual change instead of manufacturing signal from six quiet days. *This is the highest-value automation change this week — the analyst adapting to the subject.*
- **Capture-at-source, not capture-into-vault.** **Trigger:** the friction is that "add to the garden" is a separate ritual from living. **Action:** wire one existing input straight in — e.g. a phone shortcut that appends a dated line to [[notes]], or your Goodreads "read" shelf → an unstaged block in [[reading]]. **Payoff:** the vault fills as a byproduct of things you already do, instead of requiring a dedicated sit-down that keeps not happening.
- **Publish-by-default.** **Trigger:** any new entry (carried, because it's still the one change the evidence supports). **Rule:** new entries render live; the comment block is only for things you're genuinely unsure about. **Payoff:** inverts "hide until perfect" to "show unless private."
- **Staleness stamp.** **Trigger:** every entry. Append `— YYYY-MM-DD`. **Payoff:** "Currently Reading" stops silently becoming "was reading, two weeks ago." Still zero dated entries, so still worth saying — and it's the precondition for the event-triggered review above.
